There's a meme going around the internets in which intrepid nerds (and ordinary civilians) post photos of their "to-read" stack of books.  I just checked in with [a contribution to Flickr](http://www.flickr.com/photos/jonathanpberger/tags/readingstack/).  Viewing the ["readingstack" tag across the whole site](http://flickr.com/photos/tags/readingstack/) is interesting too, although it makes me feel a little queasy; like I've got a whole semester's worth of reading to do in one night.
