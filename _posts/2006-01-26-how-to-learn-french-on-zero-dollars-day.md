Pame just mentioned that she wants to learn french, and I pointed her to a [review](http://www.kk.org/cooltools/archives/000521.php) from Kevin Kelly's indispensible [Cool Tools](http://www.kk.org/cooltools/index.php).  There're 52 30-minute long segments which can be bought, borrowed from a public library, or streamed online.  Watch the whole set, one per day, three times or so, and voila--you've added another language.  Right?
