It feels like there're more and more reports of things turning nasty these days. Dave Winer of [Scripting News](http://www.scripting.com/) reports [an ugly case of Lawyers Gone Bad](http://www.scripting.com/stories/2007/03/23/russoHaleOurLawFirmSuesUs.html).  As with [the Derek & Heather mess at JPEG](http://jonathanpberger.wordpress.com/2007/05/15/bad-news-and-good-advise/), the silver lining to all of this is some good advice:
>  If a deal is worth doing, it's worth documenting with a good agreement.
Sounds about right to me.  And, happily, [this seems to have been ended with mediation](http://www.scripting.com/stories/2007/05/25/midnightUpdate.html) rather than a suit.  So congrats Dave, and hopefully this'll all be behind you.  Life's too short to deal with this kind of nonsense when you could be building stuff.
