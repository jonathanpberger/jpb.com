Last night's panel on Net Aesthetics 2.0 continued a streak of 2.0-themed events that I've recently attended. The panel was alright, but ran really long, and there was a lot of chaff to sift through. There were some good insights, especially regarding the nature of art on the internet vs. art in galleries, but i was a little disappointed with the work itself. I'm pretty crazy about most of the artists, especially Cory, but too much of the work seemed like one-liners.
