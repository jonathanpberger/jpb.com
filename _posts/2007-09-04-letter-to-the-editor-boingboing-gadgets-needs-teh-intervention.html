Dear Boingers,

I'm a longtime reader and BB fan. After the recent relaunch I eagerly added "BB Gadgets" to my daily RSS feed.

Today I dropped it.

I was annoyed by the flood of content, most of which isn't worthy of carrying the BB impreteur. BB has never been about quantity over quality, but for some reason (single editorship? "gadgets" are too numerous?) this isn't working out. I hope you'll ask around a little and see if my sentiment is shared.

Y'all are plenty smart, and I'm sure you can make a BB Gadget blog work, but this current incarnation needs some serious tweaking.

Love,
jb
