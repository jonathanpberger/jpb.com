- The Complete [Bone](http://www.boneville.com/) (in a single volume) (Jeff Smith)

- [Gary Benchley, Rock Star](http://garybenchleyrockstar.com/) (Paul Ford)

I read Ford in the [Morning News](http://www.themorningnews.org/) before I knew it was Ford, and enjoyed the column.  I think i was working at SuperCorporateWorld(TM) at the time.  Meant to pick up the book, never did, and then found if for $5 at [St. Marks](http://www.stmarksbookshop.com/NASApp/store/IndexJsp) and tore through it in about a day.  Light, but a good read.  I'll pick up the next [Ford](http://www.ftrain.com/) book i find.
