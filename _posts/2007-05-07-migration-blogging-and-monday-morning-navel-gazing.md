As I begin to get my digital house in order, its becoming apparent 1) just how all-over-the-place I am, and 2) that publishing is not just about sharing ideas, but about leaving traces.  Blame this train of thought on MC Frontalot's [Secrets From the Future](http://frontalot.com/index.php/content.php?page=lyrics&lyricid=41) (which has been stuck on repeat lately) or just general angst about having been heavily involved in blogging for the past few years, reading about it, thinking about it, but never *writing* about it in any sort of sustained way.  But it'll be nice to have a place to hang my proverbial hat.

Also: blogger doesn't export embedded code from other sites (e.g., youTube), so some of that will be done by hand, and some of that will be ignored.  C'est la vie.
