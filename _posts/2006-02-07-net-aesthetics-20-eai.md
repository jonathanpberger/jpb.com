Last night's [panel on Net Aesthetics 2.0](http://www.eai.org/eai/02_06_rhizome_pr.html) continued a streak of 2.0-themed events that I've recently attended.  The panel was alright, but ran *really* long, and there was a lot of chaff to sift through.  There were some good insights, especially regarding the nature of art on the internet vs. art in galleries, but i was a little disappointed with the work itself.  I'm pretty crazy about most of the artists, especially [Cory](http://www.beigerecords.com/cory/), but too much of the work seemed like one-liners.

I still haven't figured out how to post a txt file to Blogger, so i'll for now I'll just upload it as [a separate link](http://ye-olde-blogge.blogspot.com/2006/02/060206-net-aesthetics-20-notes.html).
