Pame just mentioned that she wants to learn french, and I pointed her to a review from Kevin Kelly's indispensible Cool Tools. There're 52 30-minute long segments which can be bought, borrowed from a public library, or streamed online. Watch the whole set, one per day, three times or so, and voila--you've added another language. Right?
